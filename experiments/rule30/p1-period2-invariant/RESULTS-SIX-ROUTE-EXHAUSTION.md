
### 2.2 Phase-gap retreat cover

Retreats are nonconsecutive, so their event windows `{t-1,t}` are disjoint.
A finite product proves the complete dangerous zero-free language

```text

```

and exact regular preimages satisfy

```text

Q^-1(P): 20 states / 2 accepting,
Q^-2(P): 1 dead state / 0 accepting.                 (3)
```

Therefore an eventless retreat is the first retreat.  One boundary credit
plus one zero block from every other disjoint window gives an injective cover
for queues of every length.

### 2.3 Local projected-support algebra

For `A=(alpha,beta,gamma)`, hard-core forcing obeys

```text
tail 2 legal iff gamma=alpha OR beta,
tail 3 legal iff gamma=NOT(alpha OR beta),
legal forced endpoint=2-alpha.                        (4)
```

the literal evaluation `A(0)`.  These identities explain exactly why the two
different projections appear in the live support lemma.
