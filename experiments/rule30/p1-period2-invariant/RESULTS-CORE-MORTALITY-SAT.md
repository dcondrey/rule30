# Active-core diagonal mortality CNF

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: **OPEN.  THE STRONGER DIAGONAL FORMULA IS UNSAT THROUGH CORE LENGTH
34, BUT NO INDUCTION IN THE LENGTH IS KNOWN.**  This is finite falsification
evidence, not a mortality proof.

## 1. Stronger exact target

Let `v` be any aligned core word of length `m` over symbols `0,1,2,3`, read
deep-to-shallow, with terminal shallow symbol `3`.  Starting from carry zero,
one exact active-core step transduces every symbol and appends a new terminal
`3`.  It passes the pin exactly when the final carry bits differ; its forced
rho bit is one minus the high carry bit.

Define `C(m,H)` to assert that some such word passes `H` steps and that the
resulting rho word has no adjacent ones.  Leading zero symbols are allowed,
so this includes padded shorter cores.  The proposed diagonal theorem is

```text
C(m,m+1) is UNSAT for every m >= 1.                  (CD)
```

This is stronger than the seed-only linear theorem.  A seed of length `n`
has stored aligned width `2n`, and stripping inert deep zeros can only shorten
it.  Therefore (CD) would force failure within `2n+1` post-seed macros.  With
the exact bilateral no-`11` reduction, it would prove the period-two theorem.

## 2. Local Boolean encoding

`core_mortality_sat.py` Tseitin-encodes only the four-state carry table.  For
input `q=(a,b)` and carry `(c,d)`, each cell asserts

```text
c' = c XOR (a OR b),
d' = d XOR (c OR a),
next symbol = (d',c').                               (1)
```

Every row starts at carry zero and appends `(1,1)`.  At its endpoint the two
clauses

```text
c OR d,
NOT c OR NOT d
```

assert the pin.  Since forced rho is `NOT c`, consecutive rows add
`c_t OR c_(t+1)`.  The only free semantic variables are the `2m` bits of the
initial word, with its last symbol fixed to `3`.

Thus `C(m,H)` is SAT exactly when the stated arbitrary-core survivor exists;
it is not a table of precomputed words.

## 3. Independent semantic validation

Every one of the 36 SAT/UNSAT thresholds through `m=7` agrees with exhaustive
enumeration of all `4^(m-1)` terminal-`3` words and independent integer
`core_step` replay.  Every decoded SAT model is replayed.

Dropping no-`11` makes `C(6,7)` SAT.  The decoded model passes seven pins and
contains adjacent forced rho ones, proving that the hard-core clauses are
load-bearing rather than redundant.

## 4. Finite diagonal result

Every diagonal instance `C(m,m+1)` for `1 <= m <= 34` is UNSAT.  The last
instance has 7,210 variables and 25,099 clauses and took about 18 seconds in
the recorded CaDiCaL run.  This sweep was used only to falsify (CD); it does
not establish a base-plus-step proof.

The exact fixed-horizon reverse cascade in `RESULTS-CORE-DISCHARGE.md` gives a
second formulation.  For horizon `H`, each input symbol acts by a permutation
on a vector of `H` carries, so `C(m,H)` is a path problem in a finite graph of
at most `4^H` states.  That graph grows with `H`.  The finite diagonal UNSAT
results have not yielded a horizon-independent invariant, interpolant, or
corner grammar.

## 5. Exact corner-peel recurrence

There is a precise reason the most literal induction does not immediately
close.  Generalize a row transduction by allowing its deep starting carry to
be a prescribed temporal sequence `beta_t`.  Remove the first deep symbol
from every row, and let `gamma_t` be the carry immediately after that removed
symbol.  If its initial symbol is `q`, then, uniformly in the remaining word,

```text
gamma_0     = tau_q(beta_0),
gamma_(t+1) = tau_swap(gamma_t)(beta_(t+1)).          (2)
```

Equation (2) follows directly from one carry-table cell: the removed symbol
in the next row is the bit-swap of `gamma_t`.  It is an all-length identity.

For the original constant-zero boundary, an active leading symbol gives
constant `gamma=2` or constant `gamma=3`.  Peeling once more gives, depending
on the next initial symbol,

```text
from beta=2:  gamma=1,1,1,...  or  3,1,1,1,...,
from beta=3:  gamma=0,2,0,2,... or 2,0,2,0,... .      (3)
```

Further applications of (2) generate additional phases and longer periods;
the obvious finite family `{constant, alternating}` is not closed.  This does
not disprove a finite symbolic grammar with richer annotations, but it rules
out a corner proof that deletes a symbol while retaining only one constant
carry mode.  A valid induction must carry the full boundary sequence, a
proved compression of it, or an amortized invariant insensitive to the mode
proliferation.

## 6. Proof obligation

The clean remaining statement is the following local triangular lemma:

> In the carry spacetime generated by a finite word ending in `3`, a right
> boundary of unequal terminal carries with no two consecutive state-`1`
> carries cannot persist for more rows than the initial word length.

A proof of that lemma would establish (CD) and the period-two rung.  A proof
must quantify over `m`; further diagonal UNSAT instances do not help unless
they expose a verified symbolic induction.

The period-two theorem and all three full Prize Problem 1 claims remain open.
