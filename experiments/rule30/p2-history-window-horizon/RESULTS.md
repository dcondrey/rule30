# Remembering past windows buys nothing, and the refutation never depended on the window's shape

Date 2026-09-19. Pre-registration `PREREGISTRATION.md` (committed as `5ca904e`,
amended as `bc3e0db` with gate G7, both before any script in this directory
existed). Analysis `history.py`, log `history.log`, record `history.json`;
lone-seed records reused from `../p2-orbit-window-horizon/`. Registered outcome A
held. This proves nothing about P2; it closes two of the three levers that
`r30-p2-orbit-window-horizon-refuted-or-vacuous` left open on route 3.

## 1. Two claims, one of them not a measurement

`r30-p2-orbit-window-horizon-refuted-or-vacuous` refutes

```text
K_t - rho = h(W_r(t+1)) - lambda * h(W_r(t))                             (1)
```

at every `r <= 18` on the first `T = 2^20` lone-seed rows, and
`r30-p2-clocked-window-horizon-refuted-or-vacuous` shows a clock of any period
through 16 spends the locality budget rather than enlarging it. That first
record's scope says it is silent on `h` depending on several past windows, on
growing or off-centre windows, and on corrections summed over the half-row.

Two of those four are closed here, and one of the two needed no new data.

## 2. Claim S: the shape of the window was never load-bearing

A statement about what the existing evidence already proves, not a new
measurement. Let `(s, t)` be a killing pair at radius `R`: `W_R(s) = W_R(t)`,
`W_R(s+1) = W_R(t+1)`, `K_s != K_t`. Any `h` whatsoever that reads only cells in
`[-R, R]` of one row is then constant on `{s, t}` and on `{s+1, t+1}`, so the
right side of (1) is equal at `s` and at `t` while the left side is not. The
shape of `h`'s support is irrelevant: off-centre window, masked or sparse subset,
a weighted sum, a parity, a half-row sum truncated to the neighbourhood.

At `R = r_pair = 18` the orbit supplies such a pair, so **every** correction
factoring through the radius-18 neighbourhood is already refuted, for every
`lambda` and every `rho`. "Non-centred windows" is not a surviving lever, and
neither is any sum over cells inside radius 18. What survives is now defined by a
number rather than a word: an `h` that reads a cell outside `[-18, 18]`.

Gate G4 confirms it mechanically at the first radius-18 killing pair, times
`544690` and `987828`, currents `54` and `474`. All 361 off-centre windows with
`|c| + r <= 18` agree at both times and at both successors. The control is met,
so the bound is not vacuous: rows `544690` and `987828` differ only at cells
`-30, -27, -23, -22, -20, 20, 21, 24, 25, 28`, every one of them outside radius
18, and their successors likewise only at cells `19` to `29` in absolute value.

## 3. Claim H: history depth is the radius budget, spent differently

A correction reading the last `d` windows of radius `r`, that is
`(W_r(t), ..., W_r(t-d+1))`, is a function of the single window `W_R(t-d+1)` with
`R = r + d - 1`, because Rule 30's light cone gives `W_(R-k)(u+k)` from `W_R(u)`.
The containment is one-way, since the past is not recoverable from the present
row, so this refutes the history class without claiming the two are equal.
Writing `u = t - L` with `L = d - 1`, (1) becomes

```text
K_(u+L) - rho = H(W_R(u+1)) - lambda * H(W_R(u)),                        (2)
```

the existing instrument with the current lagged by `L`. Both reductions carry
over unchanged: a repeated pair with `K_(s+L) != K_(t+L)` refutes (2) for every
`H`, `rho`, `lambda` (E1), and at `lambda = 1` the orbit is a single walk whose
cycle space is generated by vertex returns, each fixing
`rho = (sum_(s <= u < t) K_(u+L)) / (t - s)` (E2, complete).

Lags 0 to 12, so history depths 1 to 13, all scanned over the common time range
`u < 1,048,563` so the window data is bitwise identical across lags and only the
labels move.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| depth `d` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 9 | 11 | 13 |
| **`r_pair`** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** | **18** |

`r_pair` and `r_half` equal the birthday predictions of 18 and 19, `r_one` equals
`r_pair` as it does at `L = 0`, and every horizon equals its `L = 0` value, so
orbit minus null is `0` at every lag on all three. Outcome A held with no
deviation to explain. The `L = 0` column also equals the horizons of the
committed fixture, which is gate G2, and the common-range truncation moves
nothing: the full-range lag-zero scan gives the same 18, 18, 19.

The horizon is flat because the kill is total above `r = 13`. From `r = 14`
upward every repeated window pair is a killing pair at **every** lag: 352, 82,
23, 4 and 2 repeated pairs at `r = 14, 15, 16, 17, 18`, all of them separated by
the lagged current, and none at `r = 19`. So outcome C, a radius with repeats and
no refutation, did not occur at any lag, and the horizon is not a near miss that
a different lag might have moved.

The instrument is not merely reporting its input unchanged. `repeated_pairs` is
bitwise identical across lags by construction, which is gate G5, while
`killing_pairs` moves: at `r = 9` it is 303,692, 303,672, 303,702, 303,697,
303,694, 303,681, 303,677, 303,694, 303,655 and 303,677 over the ten lags. The
lag changes which pairs survive; it does not change how far the kill reaches.

## 5. What this closes

Route 3's lever "an identity valid only on lone-seed rows" had four escapes
listed against it. Two are now gone. History depth is not an escape: for the
measured depths `d <= 13`, a depth-`d` correction at radius `r` is refuted
whenever `r + d - 1 <= 18`, so depth 13 at radius 6 and depth 2 at radius 17 fall
alike, and depth buys locality only by spending the same `2R + 1` budget the
radius spends. Window shape is not an escape either, and never was; since both
radius-18 pairs are killing at every tested lag, Claim S applies at each of them
and the refutation there covers every support shape inside the neighbourhood, not
only windows.

Depths above 13 are not covered, and the containment does not extend for free:
Claim S separates a pair by `K_s != K_t`, so at lag `L` it needs
`K_(s+L) != K_(t+L)`, which is exactly what the sweep measures and only for
`L <= 12`.

What is left is exactly an `h` that reads a cell outside `[-18, 18]`:

- **Half-row sums.** At `t = 2^20` the half row is about `2^20` cells wide, far
  outside radius 18, so nothing here touches it. This is the one lever of the
  original four that survives intact.
- **Windows that grow past radius 18.** Two cases that the earlier scope clause
  ran together, and they are not the same. A window growing with `t` but staying
  within radius 18 is covered by Claim S at every `t`. A window that grows past
  18 is untouched, but a *single* `h` applied to windows of different widths at
  different times is a time-dependent family, and a time-dependent `h` is vacuous
  on finite data because each `t` is its own free value.

## 6. Scope

`T = 2^20`, lags `L <= 12`, and the null match is statistical rather than a
theorem. E1 is sufficient but not complete for `lambda != 1`, inherited
unchanged from the clocked run: a single return there determines `H(v)` through
`(1 - lambda^L) H(v) = ...` around a cycle rather than refuting it. E2 is
complete, so the `lambda = 1` case P2 needs carries no such gap. Claim S is a
statement about one killing pair and so is exactly as finite as the pair;
it says nothing at radii where no pair exists, which above 18 is every radius.
Nothing here bears on P2 itself, on half-row sums, on `h` depending on time
non-periodically, or on phase-dependent `lambda_a`.

- **G6** Null horizons within 2 of the birthday prediction at every lag; they in

- **G7** The light-cone step Claim H rests on, checked exactly rather than
  statistically: applying Rule 30 to the `2j+3` cells of `W_(j+1)(u)` reproduces
  the interior `2j+1` cells as `W_j(u+1)`, at every `j = 0..30` and every
  `u < T-1`. Added by amendment before any result existed, because an
  equal-windows-imply-equal-successors version would go vacuous at exactly the
  radii this run is about, and because an off-by-one in the bit indexing there
  would void Claim H silently. Its negative control was confirmed during
  development: corrupting one cell makes it fail.

The null is the construction of `../p2-orbit-window-horizon/` section 4, used
unchanged, seed `20260919`. Its currents are a permutation of the orbit's, so
lagging them is statistically a no-op and the null is flat in `L` by
construction; that is what makes orbit minus null at fixed `L`, rather than the
raw horizon, the comparison outcome A and B are decided on.
