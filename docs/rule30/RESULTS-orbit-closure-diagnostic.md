# Rule 30 vertical orbit closure: the cheap disconfirming diagnostic for R8

## Status

**MEASURED, diagnostic only.**  This document runs the first rung of route
R8 (`PATH.md`): a block-frequency test whose only power is to *disconfirm*
unique ergodicity of the vertical orbit closure of the lone-seed diagram.  It
proves nothing about prize Problem 2 in either direction.  See the closing
section for exactly what it does and does not say.

## Pre-registration (frozen 2026-08-30, before any data was generated)

**Object.**  `c = (c_t)` the lone-seed Rule 30 center column (OEIS A051023),
generated bit-parallel by the same recurrence as `experiments/rule30/center_column.py`,
first 30 terms checked against A051023.  Target length `T >= 2,000,000`.

**Metric.**  For block length `L in {1, 2, 4, 8, 12}`, window size
`W in {2^12, 2^14, 2^16, 2^18, 2^20}`, and window start depth `t0`,

```text
D(L, W, t0) = max over b in {0,1}^L of | freq(b in c[t0 : t0+W]) - 2^-L |
```

where `freq` counts overlapping length-`L` blocks (`W - L + 1` of them).
Depths: `t0 in {0, 2^10, 2^12, 2^14, 2^16, 2^18, 2^19, 3*2^18, 10^6, T - W}`,
keeping those with `t0 + W <= T`; every `W` therefore has at least 8 depths,
including `t0 = 0` and the deepest window the column allows.  The diagnostic
statistic at fixed `(L, W)` is

```text
S(L, W) = sup over t0 of D(L, W, t0).
```

**Null model.**  i.i.d. Bernoulli(1/2) columns of the same length `T`,
20 seeds (`numpy` default generator, seeds 0..19), pushed through the
identical pipeline.  Two reference scales are reported in every table:

* the analytic first-order scale `sqrt(2^-L (1 - 2^-L) / W) * sqrt(2 L ln 2)`
  (single-block s.d. times the extreme-value factor for `2^L` blocks; ignores
  overlap correlations, so a reference, not the threshold);
* the simulated band: `[min, max]` of `S_seed(L, W)` across the 20 seeds,
  plus mean and s.d.  **The seed max is the operative threshold.**

**Decision rule.**  There are 25 `(L, W)` cells.  Under the null, Rule 30's
`S(L, W)` exceeds the seed max in a cell with probability about `1/21`, so
about 1.2 exceedances are expected by chance and 4 or more has null
probability about 0.03.

* *Consistent with unique ergodicity (proves nothing):* at most 3 of 25
  cells exceed the seed max, and for each `L <= 8` the least-squares slope of
  `log2 S(L, W)` against `log2 W` lies in `[-0.65, -0.35]` (i.i.d. value
  `-0.5`).
* *Evidence against unique ergodicity, strong form of R8 killed:* either
  (A) **drift**: for `L = 1`, at the deepest depth, `|freq(1) - 1/2|`
  exceeds 3 null s.d. (`3 * 0.5 / sqrt(W)`) at two or more `W >= 2^16`; or
  (B) **plateau**: for some `L <= 8` the slope is above `-0.25` *and* the two
  largest `W` both exceed the seed max; or (C) 4 or more of 25 cells exceed
  the seed max.
* Anything else: reported as inconclusive, with the numbers.

**Kill condition for the pipeline (run before believing anything).**  The
identical pipeline on the Rule 90 lone-seed center column, generated by
simulation with `new = (row<<1) ^ (row>>1)`.  That column is `1` at `t = 0`
and provably `0` afterwards (`C(2k, k)` is even for `k >= 1`), so every
window with `t0 >= 1` must show the all-zero block at frequency 1 and
`D = 1 - 2^-L`, far outside the null band, at every depth and every `W`.  If
the pipeline fails to flag this, the pipeline is broken and nothing below
about Rule 30 is to be believed until it is fixed.

**Interpretation, fixed in advance.**  Sup-deviation shrinking like
`W^-1/2` uniformly in depth is what an i.i.d.-looking column does; it is
*consistent with* unique ergodicity of the vertical orbit closure and proves
nothing, because a finite prefix cannot certify convergence of any invariant
measure's marginal.  A depth-dependent plateau, or deep-window frequencies
drifting from `2^-L` beyond the null band, would be evidence *against*
unique ergodicity; that outcome kills the strong form of R8, retreats it to
the all-invariant-measures target, and would be a finding in its own right,
to be reported carefully.

External data point, not ours: the prize announcement reports
500,025,038 ones in the first 10^9 cells, i.e. `|freq(1) - 1/2| = 2.5e-5`
against a null s.d. of `1.58e-5` at `W = 10^9`, about 1.6 s.d.
