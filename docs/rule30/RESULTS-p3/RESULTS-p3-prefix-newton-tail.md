# Newton iteration on a genuine singleton prefix has an exact additive tail

Date: 2026-09-14. **Proved for every prefix length m>=1:** one exact global
Newton update of the true first m rows followed by zeros appends the next
true row, then continues by an additive cellular automaton. Its first
incorrect full row is exactly m+1. This does not establish the convergence
rate of a center-only query or of the particular iterates in the
[older Newton investigation](RESULTS-p3-global-newton.md). P3 remains open.

The older report and verifier are preserved. This supplement treats genuine
truncations of the singleton trajectory; its error does not require an
artificial change to two cells in an otherwise complete true diagram.

## 1. The exact series and its initial boundary

Let u(t,x) be the singleton Rule 30 diagram, with the usual update
`left XOR (center OR right)`. In coordinates r(t,j)=u(t,t-j), set

\[
r_t(X)=\sum_{j\geq0}r(t,j)X^j,\qquad
S(T,X)=\sum_{t\geq0}r_t(X)T^t.
\]

Thus r_0=1, negative j are zero, and the center bit is r(n,n). These are the
[existing right-edge coordinates](RESULTS-p3-dyadic-sections.md). Put
L=1+X+X^2 and let `\(\odot\)` denote coefficientwise multiplication in
**both** T and X. The exact equation is

\[
S=1+TLS+TX(S\odot XS). \tag{1}
\]

Indeed the coefficient update is
`r(t+1,j)=r(t,j)+r(t,j-1)+r(t,j-2)+r(t,j-1)r(t,j-2)` over F_2.
The constant 1 in (1) is the complete initial singleton, not a boundary
condition left to choose. In particular, [X^0]S=1/(1+T).

Equation (1) contains a Hadamard product, not an ordinary polynomial
product. Define its residual and its linearization at P by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal R(P)&=P+1+TLP+TX(P\odot XP),\\
\mathcal A_P(H)&=H+TLH+TX(P\odot XH+H\odot XP).
\end{aligned} \tag{2}
\]

The exact Newton update is P+H, where
\(\mathcal A_P(H)=\mathcal R(P)\). The operator \(\mathcal A_P\) is
identity plus an operator increasing T-order, so this solve exists and is
unique as a formal series. It is the same triangular Boolean Newton solve
as the older report, in moving coordinates.

## 2. Exact additive-tail theorem

For m>=1 let

\[
P_m=\sum_{0\leq t<m}r_t(X)T^t.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Newton}(P_m)
 =P_m+\frac{T^m r_m(X)}{1+T(1+X+X^2)}.} \tag{3}
\]

The quotient in (3) uses ordinary formal-series multiplication.

**Proof.** The true rows cancel the residual at every time below m, and
P_m is zero at every later time. Therefore
\(\mathcal R(P_m)=T^m r_m\). The triangular linear solve implies H is zero
below time m. Since P_m is supported strictly below m, both products
\(P_m\odot XH\) and \(H\odot XP_m\) vanish identically. Equation (2)
reduces to \((1+TL)H=T^m r_m\), proving (3).

Consequently, after retaining the first m rows, Newton gives r_m correctly
and then predicts the successive rows

\[
r_m,\quad Lr_m,\quad L^2r_m,\quad\ldots. \tag{4}
\]

Thus this particular update has a rational tail. Its existence does not
make the full nonlinear spacetime series rational or algebraic.

## 3. The next full row is wrong for every m>=1

Every true r_m, m>=1, has degree 2m and has coefficient 1 at both 2m and
2m-1. To see this, the packed update is
`G(x)=x XOR ((x<<1) OR (x<<2))`. For any nonzero x with highest bit d, the
two highest bits of G(x), at d+2 and d+1, are both 1. Start from r_0=1.

The first possible error in (3) is at time m+1. At that time its difference
from the true row is precisely

\[
X\bigl(r_m\odot Xr_m\bigr). \tag{5}
\]

The coefficient at X^(2m+1) in (5) is
`r(m,2m) r(m,2m-1)=1`. Hence the first incorrect full row is exactly m+1
for **every** m>=1. A standard claim that Newton doubles the known temporal
precision therefore fails even on these genuine singleton truncations.

The displayed error is at physical position -m, not generally the center.
This theorem does not give a center-only iteration lower bound, does not
bound the special references generated by repeatedly applying Newton from
the initial singleton approximation, and does not exclude a different
preconditioner or a query-specific solve. The input P_m also contains m
already constructed true rows; that construction is not free advice.

## 4. Exact bounded verification

and [its artifact](../../experiments/rule30/p3-prefix-newton-tail.json) check

Rule 30 truth table. A second implementation constructs every Boolean
derivative by flipping a truth-table input and performs the triangular
Newton solve. Its complete rows agree with (3), including the first-error
location and (5). The checker also verifies the disjoint temporal supports
in (2) and the resulting linear equation. Source and report hashes are
retained; the older schedule experiment is neither imported nor rerun.

The formulas and inductions prove the all-length statements. These small
controls check the implementations and conventions. No center-query speed
